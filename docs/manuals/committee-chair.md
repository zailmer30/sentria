# Committee Chair Manual

## Role purpose

Lead committee review: referrals, reports, and recommendations to the plenary.

## Committees

- View assigned committees at `/committees`.
- Manage referrals and record the committee result on the document page. Secretariat files the report for Committee Hour.

## Session floor

When seated as a member during plenary, use **Board Member** floor mode for voting and documents.

## Documents

- Access committee-referred documents per confidentiality and grants.
- Use AI compare/consistency tools as aids — not as legal determinations.

## You do NOT

- File the committee report or send a measure to second reading from the document page — secretariat files, then records the chair’s motion under Committee Hour.
- Approve final legislation in committee view — plenary process applies.
