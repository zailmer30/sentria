# Committee Member Manual

## Role purpose

Review referred documents, contribute committee input, and participate in plenary when seated.

## Committees

- Open `/committees/{slug}` for your assignments.
- Follow chair direction for report deadlines and recommendations.

## Plenary session

Use `/sessions/{id}/floor/member` during session for agenda, documents, and votes (same as board member). You can advance-read and annotate linked PDFs in the member workstation without leaving the floor.

## Private notes

Notes on documents or sessions remain visible only to you.

## AI tools

When available, AI search respects your permissions. Verify all citations against source PDFs.
