# Presiding Officer Manual

## Role purpose

Conduct session floor proceedings using presiding officer views and controls.

## Floor access

- Open `/sessions/{id}/floor/presiding` during session.
- Monitor quorum indicator (informational — **you** decide whether to proceed).
- Advance agenda items when ready; suspend/resume/adjourn per rules of procedure.

## Motions & voting

- When a member raises a motion, a live dock appears. **Recognize** gives them the floor and focuses the motion recorder, attributed to that member; **Dismiss** clears the request.
- Type the motion as spoken, then submit. Open voting when ready.
- Close voting when debate ends; declare results per your rules (electronic tallies assist but do not replace your declaration unless binding mode is enabled).

## Electronic voting notice

When `electronic_is_binding` is false, a banner reminds users that electronic tallies are advisory. Organizational policy governs binding effect.

## Offline sessions

If Wi-Fi drops, cached agenda remains on tablets. Votes queue and sync when connectivity returns. Verify tallies after reconnection before declaring results.

## You do NOT

- Rely on AI to determine quorum or vote outcomes from audio when official voting data exists.
