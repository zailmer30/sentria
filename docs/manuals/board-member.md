# Board Member Manual

## Role purpose

Participate in paperless session floor: view agenda, read documents, cast votes, take private notes.

## Floor access

- Open `/sessions/{id}/floor/member` on your assigned tablet (or desktop workstation). Opening the live paperless floor marks you **present**; the secretariat can still correct the roll call.
- The **agenda rail** lists every item. The live item is marked; select any other item to **advance-read** without leaving the floor.
- Use **Follow floor** to snap the reader back to the item currently under consideration.
- Linked PDFs open in the center pane with **EmbedPDF**. Markup is private to you and saves automatically.
- Deep-link to a specific item with `?item={agendaItemId}` (useful before the sitting opens).
- Use **My notes** for private notes on the session or on the document you are reading (owner-only).

## Motions

- Tap **Raise a motion** on the floor toolbar (or the reading pane on a workstation). There is no form — you will speak the motion.
- The chamber (member tablets, secretariat, presiding console, and hall board) sees a live alert that you seek the floor.
- Wait to be recognized. You may tap **Waiting · Cancel** to withdraw the request.
- The presiding officer or secretariat records the wording after you speak.

## Voting

When voting is open, select Yes / No / Abstain / Inhibit. On tablets, vote buttons stay in a sticky bar at the bottom so they are never buried under the PDF.

### Offline voting

If the offline banner appears:

1. Your ballot is **queued locally** — it is not lost.
2. When Wi-Fi returns, Sentria submits once (duplicate-safe).
3. Confirm your ballot shows as recorded after sync.

## Session assistant (if enabled)

AI may summarize agenda context. Always verify against official documents.

## You do NOT

- Share login credentials; each vote is attributed to your user account.
