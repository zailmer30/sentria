# Consistency guide

Prepend this block once per Google Flow job. Do not paste it into each clip prompt.

## People

Unnamed Filipino adults in a municipal hall. No likeness of a real official. No celebrities. No name plates that can be read.

- Presiding officer: a woman in her fifties, low knot, charcoal formal jacket with a barong collar, standing at a wooden rostrum.
- Board member: a man in his forties, white barong, seated halfway down a long bench.
- Secretariat: a man in his forties, navy formal shirt, sleeves neat, at a side table just off the floor. Unnamed. He is not a portrait of the account that appears on the console.
- The rest of the body: eight to twelve adults in barong and formal blouses, seated in two rows. Faces are ordinary, not famous.

## Place

A sangguniang bayan session hall. High windows, pale walls, dark wood benches, a long rostrum, a blank white projection wall. A small secretariat office next door: a desk, a paper stack, a morning window. No flags with readable seals, no coats of arms, no brand logos, no campaign colors beyond the room itself.

## Palette and light

Graphite wood, white paper, cool daylight. One note of deep blue in a folder edge or a lanyard. Red appears only as a dry-erase mark on a whiteboard in the problem scenes, never as decoration. No neon, no startup glass, no drone shots.

## Camera

16:9, 1920×1080. Locked tripod or a slow push. Eye level for people, slightly above the bench for paper. Shallow depth when a screen is in frame: the screen is angled away or soft, never readable. One action per clip. No whip pans, no text cards generated inside the clip.

## Product frames

UI clips are screen recordings, not Flow. If a Flow fallback is used, the attached product frame is locked. Flow does not redraw type, buttons, or numbers. The interface is Space Grotesk, cool gray surfaces, blue for the action, red only for a live state.
