# Voiceover script

One continuous narration for 5:04. English, except the tagline. Target 550–650 words, with silence under the hook, the tally, and the approval.

Read in a measured institutional register. No lift at the end of lines. No “you won’t believe.”

## Script

**0:00**  
*(Silence. The packet hits the bench.)*

**0:08**  
The roll is called across the hall, and the page the member needs is still in the packet.

**0:16**  
He turns the packet over, tab by tab, and the section on the floor is not there.

**0:24**  
Two people read two different numbers off the same whiteboard, and neither one yields.

**0:32**  
The count is argued in the open, and the measure waits on the bench until someone agrees.

**0:40**  
The next morning the minutes are still a stack of notes on a desk beside the hall.

**0:48**  
*(Silence. The packet is set down. The tablet comes up.)*

**0:56**  
The packet is down. The same bench is live. This is Sentria.

**1:04**  
The sitting, the vote, and the record now live in one system, on the bench where the packet was.

**1:12**  
A member reads the ordinance on the floor, with the agenda beside it and the ballot within reach.

**1:20**  
The secretariat holds the roll, the motion, and the vote from one console at the side of the hall.

**1:28**  
The hall sees the official page on the wall, the same file the member has, without the private marks.

**1:36**  
Read it, mark a passage, and keep a private note, without leaving the floor or opening another system.

**1:44**  
The note stays on this tablet. It is not the transcript, not the minutes, and not the public record.

**1:52**  
The count updates when a member opens the floor. Only present and late count. The officer decides.

**2:00**  
The secretariat sends the official PDF to the wall, and the room looks up at one copy.

**2:08**  
The room reads that copy. A highlight and a private note never leave the member's bench.

**2:16**  
A member taps once to seek the floor. The dock appears on the wall. The secretariat types what was spoken.

**2:24**  
Voting opens. The tally takes the wall where the page just was. The figure is advisory.

**2:32**  
*(Silence. One figure steps.)*

**2:40**  
The vote is cast. The debate still has to be captured before the sitting is adjourned.

**2:48**  
Lines are on the record while the sitting is open. They are not the ballots, and not the minutes.

**2:56**  
A mixer line has no name on it, until the secretariat assigns the speaker to a member of the body.

**3:04**  
A low-confidence line stays marked unverified until a person opens it and corrects the words.

**3:12**  
The assistant can show the item now on the floor. It cannot vote, and it cannot decide the sitting.

**3:20**  
Verify every line it offers against the official record before anyone treats it as the minutes.

**3:28**  
After adjournment the minutes arrive labeled draft, and the vote numbers on the page match the ballots.

**3:36**  
*(Silence on the click, then:)*  
A person approves. The machine does not finalize the record.

**3:44**  
Two measures sit side by side. The difference is marked, and it is still yours to check against the record.

**3:52**  
The network drops on the floor. The ballot stays on the tablet instead of disappearing with the signal.

**4:00**  
The connection returns. That one ballot syncs. Offline reach is the session floor only.

**4:08**  
The register is a chain. Each entry holds the one before it, and the application does not edit the row.

**4:16**  
One sitting: the marked page, the official wall, the tally, and the officer on the same floor.

**4:24**  
The minutes are approved by a person. The numbers on the page match the ballots that were cast.

**4:32**  
A published ordinance is on the public portal, where anyone can open it without a staff account.

**4:40**  
What was not published is simply not there. The portal does not offer a locked copy in its place.

**4:48**  
Sentria. Sistemang Paperless para sa Sesyong Pambatas.

**4:56**  
Request a demo. The officer decides. The record remains.

## Alternative hooks

Use these only on the first eight seconds, in place of silence plus the 0:08 line. The rest of the script stays.

1. The session has started. The record has not.
2. The packet is on the bench. The roll is still being called.
3. Someone is counting marks on a whiteboard. The measure is still on paper.
