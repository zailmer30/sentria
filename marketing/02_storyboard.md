# Sentria marketing film — storyboard

Target runtime **5:04**. **38 clips × 8 seconds.** Aspect 16:9, 1920×1080. English narration. Filipino only for the tagline *Sistemang Paperless para sa Sesyong Pambatas*.

Organization on screen: Sangguniang Panlalawigan, SP, Province of Demo. No seal, no real officials.

Floor footage is the **38th Regular Session**. Transcript proof, the draft, and the approval are the **37th Regular Session**. The edit keeps session titles and dates out of close-ups so the two sittings read as one. See assumptions in the shot list.
